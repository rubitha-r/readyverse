5. Authoritative Readiness Score Formula & 4 Benchmarks

Readiness Score = Math.round( 0.40 * simulationScore + 0.30 * learningScore + 0.30 * planScore ) Where: • simulationScore = (sum of best % scores across 4 drills) / 4 [Uncompleted drills count as 0%] • learningScore = (completed Learn modules / 6) * 100 • planScore = (completed plan checklist items / 40) * 100

8. Known Limitations & Beta Scope

⚠️ Transparent Architectural Disclosures:
  • Live AI & Voice Internet Dependency: The Ask ReadyVerse AI (Beta) conversational assistant and real-time voice capture require an active internet connection. When offline, local disaster rule grounding provides deterministic guidance.
  • Device Speech Synthesis Voices: Spoken audio playback relies on local Text-to-Speech (TTS) voices installed on the user's phone or operating system. If Tamil TTS is absent on the user's device, the app falls back cleanly with a friendly on-screen installation tip.
  • iOS Safari PWA Installation: iOS Safari does not support the Chromium beforeinstallprompt event; iOS users are shown clear step-by-step instructions (Tap Share ⎋ > Add to Home Screen).
  • No Substitution for Emergency Services: ReadyVerse is an educational preparedness tool. In an actual crisis, students must follow live campus marshal directives and call 112 immediately.
